How to use Franmyr

  1. Train: pick a program, then tap the circles next to each exercise to mark your 3 sets. Tap Finish session when you are done.
  2. Timer: choose a boxing combination or HIIT exercise, set work, rest and sets, then press Start. Turn the volume up for the beeps.
  3. Progress: enter your own measurements and tap Save measurements. Save again over time to see your chart.
  4. Language: switch between EN, FR and ES at the top of the screen.

Your data stays on this phone and browser. Clearing your browser data erases it.
